## Methodology

- CPUs: 8
- Primary workload: mixed benchmark-generation pattern.
- Threads/application: 1; memory/application: 8 MiB; iterations/thread: 17841231.
- Discovery interval: 250 ms; monitor interval: 100 ms; evaluation interval: 1000 ms.
- Warm-ups and measured repetitions are recorded in the raw CSV. Launches are sequential with measured skew because the frozen benchmark has no external pre-execution barrier.
